    Before the network goes

    • Open this page once while you have signal, then add it to your home screen. It keeps working offline afterwards.
    • Turn on battery saver and keep Bluetooth and location on. Bluetooth and Nearby Share do not need a network.
    • If one phone in your group has signal, send everyone's SOS from it: each person taps Share to a nearby phone.
    • At night or when you hear a helicopter, use Flash light + whistle. It flashes the SOS pattern (··· — — — ···).
