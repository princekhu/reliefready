    How a request becomes a delivery

    Every step is recorded, so the coordinator can explain each decision.

    1

    Ask for help

    The person picks needs and head-count; GPS works without data. The request leaves by the first channel available.

    InternetSMS (2G)BluetoothQR112
    2

    Rank

    A 0–100 priority from need type, people, infants, elderly and injured, waiting time and whether it sits in a danger zone.

    3

    Deliver

    One click turns it into a driver's stop with quantities from the demand model. The route avoids reported hazards and zones.

    4

    Escalate

    No road in? The control room requests a boat, airlift or NDRF team, and asks the State or Centre for more stock.

    Capabilities

    Built for the conditions after a flood: weak signal, submerged bridges, not enough stock.

    Offline-first SOS

    Queued on the phone, sent by SMS in one 160-character code, handed to nearby phones, or scanned by a driver.

    District map with live locations

    The Mysuru district boundary (Census 2011), vehicle positions from drivers' GPS, and your own live location.

    Impact zones

    Flooded, no-entry and caution areas block routes, raise SOS priority and decide boat or road delivery.

    Safe routing

    Road routes from OpenStreetMap, each checked against drivers' reports of submerged bridges and flooding.

    Demand model

    An explainable regression estimates daily water, food and medical kits per location and flags requests that look wrong.

    Stock requests upward

    Food, water and sanitation requests to the State, NDMA/MHA or FCI, tracked until received and added to depot stock.

    Architecture

    Small, inexpensive to run, and replaceable piece by piece.

    Web apps

    HTML, CSS, JavaScript

    Installable and offline-capable. Leaflet maps with Esri, OpenStreetMap, NASA GIBS and Bhuvan WMS layers.

    API

    Python FastAPI

    Token sign-in with roles, schema-validated records, public SOS intake, SMS webhook, documented at /docs.

    Database

    PostgreSQL

    Requests, stops, road reports, zones, vehicles and agency requests. PINs stored as salted hashes.

    Model

    Demand regression

    Trained with scikit-learn, exported as coefficients so the website and API give the same estimate.
