    How a request becomes a delivery

    Every step is recorded, so the coordinator can explain each decision.

    STEP 1

    Ask for help

    The person picks needs and head-count; GPS works without data. The request leaves by the first channel available.

    InternetSMS · 2GBluetoothQR112
    STEP 2

    Rank

    A 0–100 priority from need type, people, infants, elderly and injured, waiting time and whether it sits in a danger zone.

    STEP 3

    Deliver

    One click turns it into a driver's stop with quantities from the demand model. The route avoids reported hazards.

    STEP 4

    Escalate

    No road in? The control room requests a boat, airlift or NDRF team and tracks it to resolution.

    Capabilities

    Built for the conditions after a flood or landslide: weak signal, blocked roads, not enough stock.

    Offline-first SOS

    Queued on the phone, sent by SMS in one 160-character code, handed to nearby phones, or scanned by a driver.

    Impact zones

    Red, landslide and flooded zones block routes and raise priority. Includes the NRSC/ISRO-mapped 2024 Chooralmala landslide area.

    Safe routing

    Real road routes from OpenStreetMap, each checked against live driver reports of bridges down, flooding and landslides.

    Demand model

    An explainable regression model estimates daily water, food and medical kits per location and flags requests that look wrong.

    Fair allocation

    Every reachable place gets a minimum share first, then stock goes by priority, within vehicle capacity and hours.

    Escalation trail

    Requests to SDMA, NDRF, health or air support with a sent → acknowledged → dispatched → resolved timeline.

    Architecture

    Small, inexpensive to run, and replaceable piece by piece.

    Web apps

    HTML, CSS, JavaScript

    Installable and offline-capable. Leaflet maps with Esri, OpenStreetMap, NASA GIBS and Bhuvan WMS layers.

    API

    Python FastAPI

    Token sign-in with roles, schema-validated records, public SOS intake, SMS webhook, documented at /docs.

    Database

    PostgreSQL

    Requests, stops, road reports, zones, vehicles and agency requests. PINs stored as salted hashes.

    Model

    Demand regression

    Trained with scikit-learn, exported as coefficients so the website and API give the same estimate.
